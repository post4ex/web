Why You Need Genie App

Essential advantages engineered specifically for fast logistics operations

Camera Barcode & AWB Scanning

Scan 1D and 2D shipping labels directly using your smartphone camera with hardware acceleration—no expensive handheld laser scanners needed.

Instant Milestone Alerts

Receive real-time push notifications the moment a consignment is dispatched, reaches a transit hub, or goes out for delivery.

Offline Pincode Database

Lookup all 19,000+ Indian postal codes, carrier coverage zones, and estimated delivery times even in warehouse basements with zero network.

Volumetric Freight Estimator

Quickly compute dimensional weight calculations and estimate surface and express cargo tariffs in seconds before booking.

Direct Dispute Resolution

Raise priority investigation tickets, upload package damage photos directly from your camera, and track resolution timelines live.

Encrypted & Phishing-Proof

Eliminates risks from fraudulent SMS and WhatsApp phishing scams by providing a verified, TLS-encrypted direct gateway to Post4Ex.

How to Install on Android

STEP 1

Tap "Download APK" on the Android card above to save app-release.apk.

STEP 2

Open your browser Downloads and tap the APK. If prompted, select "Allow install from this source".

STEP 3

Tap Install. Once complete, launch Genie App and log in with your Post4Ex staff or client credentials.
