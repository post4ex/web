Common Courier Fraud Tactics & Scams

💸

The "₹5 / ₹10 Small Fee" Account-Sweeping Trap (VERY POPULAR SCAM)

Scammers send SMS or WhatsApp messages claiming: "Parcel delayed due to wrong address. Pay ₹5 to update address & deliver: link".

The Danger: Victims assume ₹5 is a harmless tiny amount. Once you enter your UPI PIN, OTP, or card details on that fake link, fraudsters capture your banking credentials and sweep out your entire bank account balance.

🚫 NEVER pay even ₹1, ₹5, or ₹10 on links sent over SMS or WhatsApp. Post4Ex charges ZERO address-update fees.
⚠️

Malicious File & APK Download Scam

Scammers send attachments via WhatsApp like Post4Ex_Track.apk, AddressUpdate.apk, or compressed .zip / .exe files.

The Danger: Installing non-image, non-PDF files installs mobile spyware/trojans that secretly read all your incoming banking OTPs and grant fraudsters full control of your device.

🛡️ SAFE RULE: NEVER open or install any file that is not a standard Image (.jpg, .png) or Document (.pdf). Post4Ex NEVER sends .apk apps.
📱

Unsolicited Phishing SMS & Fake Shortlinks

Fake text messages with shortlinks like bit.ly/track-now or tinyurl.com/post-delivery are disguised phishing traps.

🚫 Post4Ex NEVER sends delivery links to consignees. Always track directly on post4ex.in.
📞

Fake Customer Service & Customs Calls

Fraudsters call pretending to be customs or courier agents, threatening parcel cancellation unless you pay online or share an OTP.

🚫 NEVER make UPI transfers or share OTPs over phone calls.
