Our Story & Vision

PosTman by Post4Ex was created from the ground up to solve an essential operational challenge: Indian logistics and freight businesses deserved faster, smarter, and deeply integrated software.

Generic off-the-shelf tools were often clunky or expensive, struggling with the real-world complexity of multi-carrier assignments, GST reconciliation, rate calculation, and customs documentation.

Today, PosTman powers end-to-end logistics operations — from single and bulk order bookings, rate calculation, and live SSE tracking, to comprehensive financial management in The Vault — enabling logistics teams across India to operate at peak speed.

Built for Logistics by Logistics Experts

Every capability was forged through actual ground operations, carrier requirements, and Indian freight standards.
